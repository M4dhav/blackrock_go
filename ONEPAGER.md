
# BLACKROCK-GO
## An open mesh network and emergent AI for Black Rock City

---

This August, we're deploying a fully off-grid communications and AI network across the playa — powered entirely by the people who show up.

No internet. No satellites. No tokens. No central authority.
Just radios, mesh networking, distributed compute, and an oracle who speaks with the voice of the gathering.

---

### THE IDEA

Every person who carries a LoRa radio extends the mesh. Every laptop plugged into the camp network makes the AI smarter. A distributed model — sharded across every volunteered machine — grows larger and more capable the more people contribute. When the camp is full, she is profound. When the playa empties at dawn, she speaks more simply. She is always awake.

The physical face of this is a Fortune Teller installation somewhere on the open playa. Voice in. Oracle out. She is powered by the collective intelligence of everyone who chose to contribute.

---

### WAYS TO PARTICIPATE

**Carry a radio**
Flash a Heltec V4 with Meshtastic 2.7.15 and clip it to your bag. You're now a relay node. Every person you pass benefits from the range you extend.

**Download the app**
Blackrock-GO on iOS and Android. Encrypted camp channels. Location sharing with your crew. An oracle in your pocket. Works over the mesh with no internet.

**Plug in the node USB**
We're distributing bootable USB drives. Plug into any laptop at camp, reboot, and your machine's RAM joins the distributed model. Pull it out when you leave — your machine is unchanged.

**Host a gateway at your camp**
A Raspberry Pi 4 and a LoRa HAT. We'll help you configure it before the event. Your camp becomes an anchor point for the network.

**Help us build it**
Flutter developer? Python? Interested in Meshtastic, distributed inference, or mesh networking? We're building this now and there's meaningful work to do before August.

---

### THE PHILOSOPHY

Burning Man's principle of Decommodification means this is a pure gift. No earning, no tracking, no transactions of any kind. The network works because people choose to make it work.

This is also a field test. We're building a sovereign infrastructure stack — mesh networking, local AI, distributed compute — designed to operate without any platform's permission. Burning Man is the harshest possible test environment. If it runs here, it runs anywhere.

After the event, the protocol becomes the foundation of something larger. You'll have been part of proving it works.
